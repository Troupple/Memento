Memento

Memento is a free, cozy browser drawing game about remembering. Draw the world back piece by piece: grass, trees, the sun, clouds, birds, mountains, a little house and more. Everything you draw comes alive, and you can save your world as an image or GIF, or export it to keep. Code by Jambles, music by Caleb, art direction by you.
